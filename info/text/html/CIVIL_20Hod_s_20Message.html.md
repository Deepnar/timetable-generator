### HOD's Message

Dr. Seema Jagtap
Professor & Head of Department

 Department of Civil Engineering at Thakur College of Engineering and Technology was established in academic year 2014-2015.The department is known for its reputed faculty having expertise in diverse fields. Apart from academic events, the department also encourages its students to engage in extracurricular and co - curricular activities essential for development of organizational skills. The department is committed to nurture rigor amongst its students and is confident of becoming at par with the established departments in the institute and recognition at national and international level. The department continuously encourages its students to undertake mini, minor and major projects based on socio-economic issues. The Department provides consultancy services backed by experienced team proficient domain knowledge in various sectors related to Civil Engineers. Department is accredited by NBA for 3 years, 1st Cycle Accreditation w.e.f. 1st July 2021.
