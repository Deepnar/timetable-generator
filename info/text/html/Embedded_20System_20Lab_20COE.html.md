## TCET-Embedded System Lab

⦿ Establishment : Academic Year 2015-2016
⦿ Objective:
 1. To provide latest technologies knowledge for faculty of TCET.
 2. To uplift Student's knowledge for competent industrial project.
 3. To provide access of good industrial technological materials of companies having tie up with Eduvance for students of TCET.
 4. To provide platform for international certification from ARM university.

⦿ Lab benifits for students
 1. Hand on training on Industry Lab setup
 2. Certificate courses from ARM UK
 3. Internships and relevant Placement Assistantship

⦿ Placement opportunity: Larsen and Toubro, BARC, Automata Systems (Mumbai), Reanu Microelectronics, (Mumbai and Pune) Maximus Technologies (Mumbai) , EpiSkope (Mumbai), Uurmi Systems (Hyderabad)
