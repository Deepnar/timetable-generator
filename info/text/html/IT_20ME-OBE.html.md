### Outcome Based Education (P.G. Information Technology)

### Program Outcomes (POs)

-  An ability to independently carry out research /investigation and development work to solve practical problems

-  An ability to write and present a substantial technical report/document

-  Students should be able to demonstrate a degree of mastery over the area as per the specialization of the program. The mastery should be at a level higher than the requirements in the appropriate bachelor program

### Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)

-  To engage in lifelong learning, adapt to evolving technologies, and address real-world and societal challenges through sustainable and responsible engineering practices

-  To demonstrate professional competence with effective communication, leadership, ethical practices, and to contribute in industry and academia

-  To apply advanced knowledge, research skills, and modern tools to design and develop innovative solutions in emerging domains such as Information Communication Technology, Artificial Intelligence, and Web Application Development
