### TCET / MME - ASME
[IMG: ASMETCET]

 ASME-TCET is the student chapter of the esteemed American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME), at Thakur College of Engineering and Technology (TCET), Mumbai. ASME-TCET student forum was formed in academic year 2013-14 and was inaugurated by Scientific Officer S.P. Srivastava (BARC, Mumbai). ASME-TCET is dedicated to nurturing aspiring mechanical engineers and empowering them to reach new heights in their academic and professional journeys. It serves as a vibrant platform for mechanical engineering students to engage, network, and thrive in an intellectually stimulating environment.

The committee provides opportunities to connect with industry experts to enhance technical skills, expand industry awareness, and broaden perspectives, through a wide range of events, seminars, workshops. From technical seminars and design competitions to industrial visits, it continuously strives to provide a holistic learning experience that complements the academic curriculum.

# Description of Events

1. Seminar – Career in Defence Sector

- Cmdr. Vijay Pratap Singh Sir introduced students to various wings of the Indian Armed Forces and their induction process.

- Some of the topics covered were: Paramilitary forces, Central Armed Police Forces, DRDO, CBI, etc.

- 128 students attended the seminar.

- Purpose: impart knowledge and motivate students to consider a career in the Defense sector.

2. Seminar - Innovation to Start-up

- Focused on formation, workings, and sustainability of Startups in India.

- Seven core elements required for a startup was discussed.

- Main focus of seminar was to help students convert innovative ideas into budding Startups.

- 167 students participated in the seminar.

3. Zephyr 2022-23

- Zephyr 2022 was conducted by all professional bodies on 6th, 7th and 8th of October.

- The Technical Event consisted of various Workshops and Fun Games

- Models of all the teams under SAE were displayed in Zephyr 2022. The teams that participated were:

- Team Mavericks

- Team Eclipse

- Team Photon

- Team Technocrats

- Team Aeronix

- Nirman Hyperloop Team

4. Workshop - Programming essential for Automation Engineers

- The Workshop was conducted from 20th to 25th February, 2023.

- Programming lies at the heart of automation, enabling engineers to design, control, and optimize automated systems efficiently.

- Introduction to key programming concepts through interactive demonstrations and hands-on exercises was done.

- Python language was preferred for clearing concepts

- Total 100+ students attended the Workshop.

5. Seminar - Gas Turbines

- Presented by Mr. Subodh Shrivastava, an experienced professional

- Application of gas turbine cycles in industries like Power Generation, oil and gas, aviation, and marine propulsion

- Detailed explanation of Jet propulsion system in aviation

- Benefits, drawbacks, and environmental impact of each type of gas turbine discussed

- Emphasized importance of efficiency and sustainability in gas turbine applications

6. Webinar - Alumni Interaction

- Mr. Balaji Murthy was the speaker for Alumni interaction.

- He enlightened students with his experience and knowledge about his involvement in research activity.

- Introduced students on topics like: control engineering, battery research, E-machines, Car 2X, Hardware and Software in the loop

- The Webinar was attended by SE, TE, BE students of mechanical department.
