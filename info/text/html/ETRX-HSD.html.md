###### Electronics and Computer Science

### Activity Based Learning

Activity based Learning comes under Holistic and Multidisciplinary Education(HME) Teaching scheme.

- Objective of Activity based Learning is to prepare students for Leadership and Excellence in their life roles by continually engaging students in both individual and team activities that explore important issues or phenomena, use multiple media and technologies, create products that embody the results of the students explorations, and call the students to explain their work and products to adult and student audience.

- Further the course also aims to develop the Society Sensitive Citizens by creating awareness among students and take up the initiatives in the Activity mode for the needy.

- The course intends to deliver the understanding of the concepts of critical thinking, encourage the students to look beyond their textual knowledge, establish the relationship between theory and the applications of the learned concepts.

- It also intends to address the social issues and help the society in the area of work.

### Project Based Learning

Project Based Learning is a credit course developed with an objective to explore various opportunities in different sectors and domains, Identify and address prospects and challenges in them with Collaborating Learning of Core subjects and ABL (Activity Based Learning), PS (Professional Skills) and ESD (Employment Skills Development). It provides deeper engagement and interaction with learning content. It is also fostering 21st century skills like collaboration and communication. This HME activity is conducted for SE and TE Students from III Sem to VI Sem. The activity is conducted in the Department of Electronics and Computer Science. Specific labs are allotted to the students for the same. It is usually conducted during the 2nd week from the commencement of the semester along with other HME activities. The activity is conducted in two parts, one week: During this the background of the theme is given. Complete Semester: Implementation of the project is done. The main aim of this is product development, so the PBL activity is planned in stages starting from TRIZ 40 Inventive Principle: Based on Graduate Attributes in sem III, Design thinking covering INSPIRATION: RESEARCH: IDEATION: IMPLEMENTATION in Sem IV, Project management in session V and Finally on 7Cs of product development in sem VI.

### Research Based Learning (RBL)

Research based Learning is focused to engage the learner in research by upgrading domain knowledge by participation in technical quiz and debate, critical thinking, innovative idea generation and technical writing. It provides deeper engagement and interaction with learning content. It is also fostering 21st century skills like collaboration and communication. This HME activity is conducted for TE and BE Students from V Sem to VIII Sem. The activity is conducted in the Department of Electronics and Computer Science. Specific labs are allotted to the students for the same. It is usually conducted during the 2nd week from the commencement of the semester along with other HME activities. The activity is conducted in four parts. Sem V: RBL I, Sem VI: RBL II, Sem VII: RBL III, Sem VIII: RBL VI. The main objective is product development. This course is focused to engage the learner in research using critical thinking, problem solving, coding and technical writing related to upcoming latest technologies.
