### HOD's Message

Dr. Udhhav D. Nimbalkar
 Associate Professor & Head of Department

The Mechanical Engineering department established in 2012-13, Department is dedicated for providing a world-class education that emphasizes innovation, research, and professional skill development with an undergraduate intake of 60 students, a postgraduate intake of 18 students, and a Ph.D. intake of 10. We offer a comprehensive range of programs designed to meet the diverse needs and aspirations of our students. Mechanical engineering is a vital field that addresses some of the most pressing challenges of our time, including renewable energy, sustainable design, advanced manufacturing, and robotics. Our curriculum is meticulously crafted to blend theoretical knowledge with practical skills, ensuring our graduates are well-prepared to excel in their careers.

 Industries of all varieties do require a large number of mechanical engineers. So there is unlimited scope for employment and higher study to secure a bright future for those opting to become Mechanical Engineers. I wish success in your endeavor to join us on the journey of quality education & to have a great learning experience with my excellent, loving & caring team.

 We are proud to have received second cycle NBA accreditation for Tier-I, a testament to our commitment to excellence in education and research. Our state-of-the-art laboratories, cutting-edge research facilities, and dedicated faculty—leaders in their respective fields—provide an enriching environment for our students.

Professional skill development is a cornerstone of our educational approach. We actively collaborate with industry leaders and research institutions to ensure our curriculum remains relevant and cutting-edge. Our programs are designed to develop critical thinking, problem-solving abilities, and practical expertise, empowering our graduates to thrive in a competitive global environment.

Student development is further enhanced through active participation in three professional bodies: ASME (American Society of Mechanical Engineers), SAE (Society of Automotive Engineers), and ISHRAE (Indian Society of Heating, Refrigerating, and Air Conditioning Engineers). These affiliations provide our students with valuable networking opportunities, professional development, and specialized training, enabling them to earn honor's degree with specializations.

Thank you for visiting our website. We look forward to your involvement in our vibrant community.
