### Outcome Based Education (P.G. Electronics & Tekecommunication Engineering)

### Program Outcomes

- An ability to independently carry out research /investigation and development work to solve practical problems.

- An ability to write and present a substantial technical report/document.

- Students should be able to demonstrate a degree of mastery over the area as per the specialization of the program. The mastery should be at a level higher than the requirements in the appropriate bachelor program.

-

### Program Specific Outcomes

- Students will be able to demonstrate knowledge acquired in the field of communication engineering, signal processing, embedded systems, semiconductor technology, cyber security and information technology.

- Students will be able to design projects using modern design tools to provide solution to real world problems using optimal resources.

- Students will be able to demonstrate knowledge, skill and attitude to carry out research in the field of Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering.
