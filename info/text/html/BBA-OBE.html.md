### Program Educational Objectives

| PEO 1: | Leadership Development- To Cultivate leadership skills and ethical decision-making abilities to excel in diverse business environments. |
| PEO 2: | Global Perspectives -To Foster a global mindset to understand international markets, cultures, and business practices. |
| PEO 3: | Strategic Thinking- To develop strategic thinking and problem-solving capabilities to address complex business challenges effectively. |
| PEO 4: | Innovation and Entrepreneurship- To encourage innovation and entrepreneurial spirit to create value and drive organizational growth. |
| PEO 5: | Professional Excellence- To equip students with professional skills, communication proficiency, and teamwork capabilities for successful careers in the business world. |

### Programmed Specific Outcomes:

| PSO 1: | Apply foundational knowledge of business theories, principles, and practices to analyze and solve business problems effectively. |
| PSO 2: | Utilize critical thinking skills to evaluate complex business situations, identify issues, and develop innovative solutions. |
| PSO 3: | Integrate Ethical considerations into Business Directions and practices, demonstrating a commitment to corporate social responsibility and sustainability. |

### Programme Outcomes

| PO 1 | Knowledge of Business Concepts: "Students will apply core business concepts such as market dynamics, financial analysis, and strategic planning to analyze and solve real-world business scenarios." |
| PO 2 | Critical Thinking and Problem Solving: "Students will systematically approach complex business problems, using logical reasoning, data analysis, and creative solutions to make well-informed decisions." |
| PO 3 | Effective Communication Skills: "Students will effectively communicate ideas and information through well-structured reports, engaging presentations, and tailored messaging to diverse audiences." |
| PO 4 | Teamwork and Leadership: "Students will excel in collaborative team environments, contribute meaningfully to group projects, and demonstrate leadership by managing team dynamics and motivating peers." |
| PO 5 | Ethical and Social Responsibilities: "Students will integrate ethical considerations into business decisions, promoting corporate social responsibility, environmental sustainability, and fair practices." |
| PO 6 | Global and Cultural Awareness: "Students will navigate and respect cultural differences, adapt business strategies for international contexts, and address global challenges with sensitivity and understanding." |
| PO 7 | Entrepreneurship Mindset and Innovation: "Students will identify and capitalize on entrepreneurial opportunities, develop innovative solutions, and exhibit resilience and creativity in business ventures." |
| PO 8 | Professional Development and Adaptability: "Students will actively pursue ongoing professional growth, demonstrate adaptability in the face of changing business environments, and continuously seek opportunities for self-improvement." |
| PO 9 | Quantitative and Analytical Skills: "Students will apply quantitative methods, data analysis, and financial modeling to interpret numerical information and drive data-driven business decisions." |
| PO 10 | Strategic Management: "Students will develop and implement strategic plans, analyze competitive environments, and make strategic decisions to achieve organizational goals and enhance market positioning." |
