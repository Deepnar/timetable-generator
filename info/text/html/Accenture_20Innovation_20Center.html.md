## Thakur-Accenture Innovation Centre

⦿ Establishment : Academic Year 2015-2016
⦿ Objective:
 1.To deploy advanced database management system and configuration
 2.To deploy Accenture ERP Software
 3.To Conduct mini/major project related to ERP/ ADBMS.

⦿ Lab benifits for students
 1.Hands on experience of Accenture ERP
 2.In-house training of placed students
 3.Availability of high end resources like IBM, Blade Server and ORACLE DBMS SW
 4.Centre of professional training, and Accenture's Head Start Foundation Programme, etc.
⦿ Placement opportunity: Accenture, ORACLE, Infosys, Zensar Technology, I-Gate.
