## Rules For Progression

## [IMG: one] BE/B.Tech/B.Voc

####  Eligibility Criteria for Admission to Subsequent Higher Year

- A candidate shall be allowed to keep term for Semester II irrespective of grades obtained in each course of Semester-I.

- A candidate shall be allowed to keep term for Semester III if s/he passes each of Semester I & Semester II.
OR
- S/he fails in not more than eight heads of passing of which not more than five shall be in End Semester Examinations of Semesters I & II taken together.

- A candidate shall be allowed to keep term for Semester IV irrespective of grades obtained in each course of Semester-III.

- A candidate shall be allowed to keep term for Semester V if s/he passes each of Semester I, Semester II, Semester III & Semester IV.
OR
- S/he has passed in all heads of Semester I & II and fails in not more than eight heads of passing of which not more than five shall be in End Semester Examinations of Semesters III & IV taken together.

- A candidate shall be allowed to keep term for Semester VI irrespective of grades obtained in each course of Semester-V.

- A candidate shall be allowed to keep term for Semester VII if s/he passes each of Semester I, Semester II, Semester III Semester IV, Semester V & Semester VI.
OR
- S/he has passed in all heads of Semester I, II, III & IV and fails in not more than eight heads of passing of which not more than five shall be in End Semester Examinations of Semester V & VI taken together.

- A candidate shall be allowed to keep term for Semester VIII irrespective of grades obtained in each course of Semester-VII.

## [IMG: one] ME/M.Tech

####  Eligibility Criteria for Admission to Subsequent Higher Year

- A learner who has not passed semesters I and II examination in all respects shall be also allowed to keep the term in semester III.

- No learner shall be allowed to appear for the Dissertation and viva voce examination unless s/he has satisfactorily kept the terms for the semester I, II and III and passed fully in semester I, II and III examinations.

## [IMG: one] BCA

####  Eligibility Criteria for Admission to Subsequent Higher Year

-  A candidate shall be allowed to keep term for Semester II irrespective of grades obtained in each course of Semester-I.

-  A candidate shall be allowed to keep term for Semester III if s/he passes each of Semester I & Semester II.

- OR

-  S/he fails in not more than seven heads of passing of which not more than four shall be in End Semester Examinations of Semesters I & II taken together.

-  A candidate shall be allowed to keep term for Semester IV irrespective of grades obtained in each course of Semester-III.

-  A candidate shall be allowed to keep term for Semester V if s/he passes each of Semester I, Semester II, Semester III & Semester IV.

- OR

-  S/he has passed in all heads of Semester I & II and fails in not more than seven heads of passing of which not more than four shall be in End Semester Examinations of Semesters III & IV taken together.

-  A candidate shall be allowed to keep term for Semester VI irrespective of grades obtained in each course of Semester-V.

## [IMG: one] MCA

####  Eligibility Criteria for Admission to Subsequent Higher Year

-  A learner shall be allowed to keep term for Semester II irrespective of grades obtained in each course of Semester I.

-  A learner shall be allowed to keep term for Semester III if s/he passes each of Semester I and Semester II.

- OR

-  S/he fails in not more than four heads of passing of Semester I and Semester II taken together.

-  A learner shall be allowed to keep term for Semester IV irrespective of grades obtained in each course of Semester III.

## [IMG: one] BBA

####  Eligibility Criteria for Admission to Subsequent Higher Year

- A learner who passes all papers in Semester I can proceed to Semester II.

- Semester I → II: Students can progress irrespective of backlogs.

- Semester II → III: Allowed if all subjects in Semesters I & II are passed or if failed in not more than two subjects in each Semester (A learner failing in up to four subjects across Semester I and II can still move to the second year)

- Semester III → IV: Allowed regardless of the number of failures in Semester III.

- Semester IV → V: Allowed only after passing all First-Year subjects and failing in not more than two subjects in each Semester of III & IV (A learner failing in up to four subjects across Semester III and IV can still move to the third year)

- Semester V → VI: Allowed only after passing all subjects up to Second-Year and failing in not more than two subjects in Semester V.

- If a learner fails to attend internal exams due to medical condition, they can appear in supplementary exams with a valid medical certificate.

- Once a learner passes all semesters of the BBA program, they cannot re-register to improve their results.

## [IMG: one] MBA

####  Eligibility Criteria for Admission to Subsequent Higher Year

- i) A learner who passes all papers in Semester I can proceed to Semester II.

- j) Semester I → II: Students can progress irrespective of backlogs.

- k) Semester II → III: Allowed if all subjects in Semesters I & II are passed or if failed in not more than two subjects in each Semester (A learner failing in up to four subjects across Semesters I and II can still move to the second year)

- l) Semester III → IV: Allowed regardless of the number of failures in Semester III.

- m) If a learner fails to attend internal exams due to medical condition, they can appear in supplementary exams with a valid medical certificate.

- n) Once a learner passes all four semesters of the MBA program, they cannot re-register to improve their results.
