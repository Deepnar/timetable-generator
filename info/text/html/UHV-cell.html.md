### [IMG: six] About UHV Cell

“Integrating Ethical Values with Technical Excellence.”
 In today’s fast-moving world, where the focus is largely on upskilling technical abilities, the essence of human values often gets overlooked. At TCET, the Universal Human Values (UHV) Cell has been established to complement professional education with value-based learning. It aims to nurture ethical understanding, a humanistic outlook, and a sense of social responsibility among students. Through workshops, orientation programs, and interactive sessions, the Cell helps learners gain clarity about life goals, build harmony in relationships, and adopt sustainable ways of living. By integrating human values with technical knowledge, the UHV Cell strives to prepare students to become not only competent professionals, but also responsible and compassionate individuals dedicated to societal and environmental well-being.

### [IMG: six] Objective of UHV Cell

⦿ To integrate ethical values with technical education, thereby nurturing competent, responsible, and compassionate professionals who contribute positively to society and promote harmony with self, others, and nature.

⦿ To promote harmony in human relationships by fostering trust, respect, and responsibility within the family, society, and the larger human community.

⦿ To nurture social awareness, inclusiveness, and ethical responsibility for building a just and peaceful society.

⦿ To encourage sensitivity towards nature by adopting sustainable practices and living in balance with the environment.

⦿ To prepare students to live with clarity and purpose, contributing to the well-being of self, family, society, and nature as an integrated whole.
