### TCET / CSE - OWASP
[IMG: TCET]

The OWASP TCET Student Chapter was founded in July 2023 with an aim to establish a Cybersecurity community at TCET and encouraging students and faculty enthusiastic about exploring and sharing the knowledge of cybersecurity and familiarize everyone with the necessary information to protect themselves in this digital world.

##### Vision

To provide world-class cybersecurity education, cultivate ethical practices, and conduct cutting-edge research to enhance security. We aim to equip individuals with the knowledge and skills they need to become cybersecurity experts to ensure digital safety in an ever-evolving digital landscape.

##### Mision

To become a global leader in cyber security education & research, fostering a culture of excellence and ethical practice in the digital realm. We aspire to shape the future of cyber security by empowering individuals with knowledge, skills and a commitment to safeguard the digital world.
