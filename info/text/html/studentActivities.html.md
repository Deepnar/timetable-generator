## TCET Student Development and Welfare Association (TSDW)

To provide a platform for co-curricular and extracurricular activities, the TCET Student Council was formed in 2004-05 under Mumbai University guidelines. As the representative body of the students, it oversees major events like SOJOURN, TSpark, Zephyr, HACAKANOVA, and LeaderCon, with support from faculty. In 2019, the council evolved into the TCET Student Development & Welfare Association (TSDW) after the institute gained autonomy. TSDW coordinates a wide range of activities, including sports, cultural events, and student development programs, aimed at fostering holistic growth. The General Secretary, supported by eight secretaries, drives these initiatives, ensuring active student participation and effective event outcomes.
