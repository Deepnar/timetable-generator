### Growth and Career opportunities in BBA

A Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) is an undergraduate degree that provides foundational knowledge and skills in business management, finance, marketing, and Human Resource. BBA course offers diverse career opportunities across fields like management, marketing, finance, human resources, sales, and entrepreneurship.

Graduates can start in roles such as business development Executive, marketing executive, Financial Executive or HR Executive. There are various courses that BBA graduates can undertake to enhance their careers and skill sets, including , a Master in Business Administration (MBA), Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM) , Masters in Management studies ( MMS), ACCA,CFA , etc
