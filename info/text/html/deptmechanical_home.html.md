### Growth and Career opportunities in Mechanical Engineering

 Thakur College of Engineering and Technology (TCET)’s BE in Mechanical Engineering degree program was designed around standards set by the All-India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). It is the first and only bachelor’s degree program of its kind in Mumbai of Higher Education — claiming the most appropriate choice for higher education for a growing future. NEP 2020 has been implemented that proposes a 4-year multi-disciplinary bachelor's degree in an undergraduate program with multiple exit options. Four-year Program consists of Design, Development, Production, Installation and Operations of all Mechanical Systems of Industries and also involves Maintenance and Repairs attributes of mechanical systems.

Mechanical Engineering has wide scope in application from design,thermal,manufacturing & Automation like Automobile,Robotics,Power Plant like Nuclear,thermal,solar wind,power plants,oilk & gas where piping,boilers,pumps,compressors were used extensively If students were taken admissions they may go to higher education by qualifying GATE exam or could get admission in foreign university by Qualifying GRE,toeful Exams Gate it is department also offers foreign Language course in German,French & Japanese for placement in foreign university. Mechanical Engineers Develops Solutions to problems in Society within the Constraints of economy & Environmental that could be in power Requirements, or travel Requirements etc. Mechanical Engineers Design, Develop & upgrade the products, system & processes in every walk of human life At TCET Mechanical Engineering Students were Crafted for Successful Carreers With Social Sensitivity & also promote culture of Research so that students were lean towards self learning & life long Learning Flow.
