## IIT BOMBAY REMOTE CENTRE

Establishment : Academic Year 2012-13
Objective :
 1. To provide a chance to every learner to acquire highest quality education.
 2. To empower teachers, through workshops conducted for thousands of teachers at one go, using a unique blend of technology and an innovative pedagogy.
Expected Outcome :
 1. Participation in IIT Bombay Remote Centre leads to enhancement in the understanding of teachers in respective course, and improvement in class room teaching. The use of new technologies and tools helps to further enhance the educational experience. After the course the teachers will be able to use open source contents, technologies and tools for laboratory use.
 2. Till date the Remote Centre has conducted 21 workshops. The participants for the workshop are TCET faculties as well as the engineering faculties from all over India. We have also conducted the workshops for engineering students.
