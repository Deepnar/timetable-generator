## About Departmental Library

TCET has 11 departments & every department has its departmental library. The Departmental Library is managed by departmental coordinators under the guidance of HODs. This library service unit with collection on an average of 600 books and 600 project reports (In the branch Computer, IT, Electronics & Telecommunication, Mechanical, E&CS)

The Departmental Library is organized into the respective department primarily to serve the needs of that particular department’s academic areas. Following new branches has its collection in Departmental Library.

- ES&H = 320

- Civil = 310

- IOT = 70

- MME = 20

- AI&DS = 27

- AI&ML = 10

- CSE = 10
