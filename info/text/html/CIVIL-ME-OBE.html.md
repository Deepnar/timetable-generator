### Outcome Based Education (P.G. Civil Engineering)

###  Program Outcomes

- PO1: Ability to effectively & efficiently apply the knowledge to solve complex engineering problems.

- PO2: Ability to demonstrate expertise in solving specific industry related problems

- PO3: Ability to design & develop products/services/solutions which benefits society at large.

- PO4: Ability to write and present technical papers in reputed journals.

- PO5: Ability to analyse impact of engineering solutions in global, economic, environmental & Societal context

###  Program Specific Outcomes

- PSO1: Apply knowledge of computing to classic problems of various domains.

- PSO2: Develop life-long learning attitude by acquiring multidisciplinary knowledge for satisfying global requirements.

- PSO3: Acquire knowledge of construction management, professional ethics ,IPR & patenting, digital business & environment management to have all round personality in order to become valued and responsible professionals.
