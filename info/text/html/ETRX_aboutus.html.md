### Department of Electronics and Computer Science

Electronics Engineering is started from the academic year 2008-2009 with an intake of 60. The 1st batch of final year students passed out in the academic year 2012-13. Now the Department has renamed as Electronics and Computer Science with the intake of 60.Department is striving to set new academic standards with wide acceptability of their graduates in the industry globally. Department has been accredited for 3 years by NBA w.e.f 1st July 2016 in the 1st cycle and reaccredited on 1stJuly 2022 with 3rd cycle.

 The department has a professional body IETE, where the students get the opportunity to explore, enhance and showcase their talents to inspire the students apart from this we also organize regular training in software & hardware, workshops, seminars and International Conferences annually named MultiCON W.

# Major Subjects

Basic Engineering: Analog Electronics , Principles of Control Systems (PCS), Advanced Engineering Mathematics (Applied Mathematics III and Applied Mathematics IV) , Microprocessors and Computer Organization (MCO) , Principles of Electronics Communication Systems (PECS), Digital Circuits Design (DCD), Data Structures(DS), Data Base Management System (DBMS), Business Communication and Ethics etc.

Core Engineering : Instrumentation System Design(ISD), Computer Networking and Communication, Microcontrollers and Applications (MCA),Theory of Computer Science. VLSI design, Digital Control system (DCS), Computer Organization and Architecture etc.

Advanced Engineering:Computer Organization (CO), Instrumentation System Design (ISD), Digital Signal Processing (DSP), Advanced Networking Technologies (ANT), CMOS VLSI Design (CVLSI), Power Electronics (PE), Artificial Intelligence and Machince Learnning (AIML), Embedded System and RTOS, Wireless Communication (WC), IC Technology (ICT), Internet of Things (IoT) , Analog and Mixed VLSI Design etc.

Application based :Robotics, Digital Image Processing, Biomedical Electronics , Neural Network and Fuzzy Logic , MEMS Technology , etc.
