### Innovative Teaching Practices

 The Department of Engineering Sciences and Humanities strives to deliver 21st century pedagogy that provides an inclusive environment for all students by integrating a variety of education, research, and other opportunities to increase knowledge and skills in order to meet students' scientific and technical goals. The department fosters a culture that supports and embraces the ideals of diversity, inclusivity, and innovation in campus life, curriculum, and the application of knowledge to real-world problems in a global context for the holistic development of technocrats. As a result, learning is delivered through a variety of teaching strategies such as Curriculum Based Learning (CBL), Aptitude and Attitude Development (AAD), and numerous Bridges courses.
