 The department of Information Technology, started its journey in the year 2002 and is committed to deliver the program with rigor and with active industry participation. Department has 180 seats intake at first year and 18 seats as lateral entry at 2nd year for engineering diploma students. The department believes in student centric approach. Its dedicated team of faculty members inculcate relevant knowledge, skills and attitude in students to become successful professionals. The U.G. programme is accredited by National Board of Accreditation (NBA), New Delhi for three years w.e.f. 16.09.2011 in the first cycle. UG Programme has been re-accredited for 3 years by NBA w.e.f 1st July 2022 in fourth cycle. Also the programme is permanently affiliated with University of Mumbai since AY 2015-16 onwards.

# Major Subjects

Basic Engineering: Logic Design, Principles of Communication, Database Management Systems, Java Programming Lab, Data Structure and Analysis, Computer Organization & Architecture.

Core Engineering: Cryptography & Network Security, Internet Programming, Computer Networks, Operating System, Automata Theory, Computer Graphics & Virtual Reality, Software Engineering.

Advanced Engineering: Advance Database Management System, Data Mining & Business Intelligence, Internet of Thing, Software Project Management, Storage Network Management & Retrieval.

Application based : Wireless Technology, Cloud Computing, Intelligent System, Computer Simulation and Modelling, e-Commerce & e-Business, Big Data Analytics, Software Testing & Quality Assurance,RPA,FSD-Industry Relevant Courses.
