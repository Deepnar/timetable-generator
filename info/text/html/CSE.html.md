### Growth and Career opportunities in Computer Science & Engineering (Cyber Security)

 In today's interconnected world, the risk of cyber-attacks has significantly increased. Whether you are interested in pursuing a career in cybersecurity or simply concerned about protecting yourself online and on social media, gaining proper knowledge and skills is crucial. By studying cybersecurity, you will not only learn how to safeguard your privacy online but also gain a deeper understanding of the complex challenges faced by companies, government agencies, and educational institutions. Given the rapidly evolving nature of the digital landscape, there is a growing demand for cybersecurity professionals, making it a promising field to explore. To fulfil sophisticated and continuously evolving cybersecurity trends which are aimed at providing security over sensitive data, TCET introduces B.E. Computer Science and Engineering in Cyber Security course. This course will help students to pursue their careers in cybersecurity which will help them to possess the required skillset to protect valuable, sensitive data and also to predict future vulnerabilities and get industry ready.

Qualified professionals in the field of cybersecurity can indeed expect promising career prospects. According to a report by Fortune Business Insights, the global cybersecurity market is anticipated to reach a size of USD 424.97 billion by 2030. This projected growth reflects the increasing recognition of the critical importance of cybersecurity across industries and the need for robust security measures in the digital age. Choosing cybersecurity as a specialization within Computer Science engineering can indeed provide students with a strong foundation and specific knowledge in the field. It allows individuals to develop the necessary technical skills and expertise required to pursue a career in cybersecurity. The job market for cyber security professionals is growing at a quick rate. This rate is quicker than other lines of work. The job opportunities and openings in the field are wide and the education needed to land these jobs is also specific.
