### TCET / AI&DS - S4DS
[IMG: S4DS]

 The Society for Data Science (S4DS) at Thakur College of Engineering and Technology (TCET) is a student-driven professional body under the department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (AIDS). Inaugurated on September 1,2023, it focuses on data-driven learning, networking, and technical skill development through workshops, datathons, and seminars. Our mission is to empower students and data enthusiasts with the knowledge and skills required to thrive in the dynamic field of data science.

 We offer hands-on workshop training sessions, and mentorship programs to equip our members with practical data science skills. Connect with fellow data enthusiasts, industry experts and researchers to broaden your horizons in the world of data. Participate in real-world data projects and competitions to apply your knowledge and gain valuable experience. Come, join and gain experience !
