### Growth and Career opportunities in MBA

A Master of Business Administration (MBA) is an master degree Program that provides foundational knowledge and exploring specializations in finance, marketing, Human Resource and operations. MBA course offers 360 degree holistic approach in curriculum including Value added courses, Smart Classrooms, Tech Umbrella, Industry practices, Mentoring by Experts and Computer Labs.

Masters can start in roles such as Content Manager, Entrepreneur, Research Scholar, Training and Development Manager, Human Resources Manager, Supply Chain Manager, Operations Manager, Product Manager, Marketing Manager, Investment Banker, Risk Analyst and Wealth Manager.

There are four specializations in MBA

- Finance: Investment Banking, Financial Analysis, Corporate Finance

- Marketing: Brand Management, Market Research, Digital Marketing

- Human Resource​: Human Resources Manager, Training and Development Manager​

- Operations Management: Supply Chain Management, Logistics, Project Management
