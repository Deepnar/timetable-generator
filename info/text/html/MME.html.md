| 🌟 Life @ MME (Mechanical & Mechatronics Engineering)  Life in the Department of Mechanical and Mechatronics Engineering (MME) is an exciting blend of creativity, innovation, and technology. It offers students a dynamic environment where traditional mechanical engineering principles meet modern automation, robotics, and smart system design. 🔧 Academic Excellence The curriculum of MME is designed to nurture analytical thinking and practical problem-solving. Students engage with subjects like Mechanical Design, Robotics & Automation, Control Systems, and Additive Manufacturing (3D Print), gaining a strong foundation for industry and research. ⚙️ Hands-on Learning Workshops, laboratories, and real-time projects form the heart of MME life. Students get opportunities to work with CAD Modelling, CNC machines, 3D printers, PLC systems, Circuit Design and Industrial robots, bridging the gap between theory and practical application.  🤖 Innovation & Research Culture The department encourages innovation through projects, mini competitions, and participation in national-level technical events, hackathons, and research paper presentations. Students frequently contribute to cutting-edge work in fields like autonomous vehicles, Industry 4.0, renewable energy, and sustainable manufacturing. 👥 Clubs & Activities MME students are actively involved in departmental clubs like:  MARS Club Open AI Club TRS  These clubs foster teamwork, creativity, and leadership through events, seminars, and technical fests. 🌍 Industry Exposure Regular industrial visits, internships, and expert lectures keep students connected with the evolving trends of the manufacturing and automation sectors. Collaborations with industries help students gain practical insights and prepare for professional roles. 💡 Holistic Development Life @ MME is not just about machines — it’s about people, passion, and purpose. Through soft-skill training, value education, and teamwork-based learning, students develop confidence, ethics, and a strong sense of responsibility toward society and sustainability. 🎯 The MME Spirit From brainstorming design solutions to full phase lab experiments, MME life is all about curiosity, collaboration, and commitment. It shapes students into innovators, leaders, and responsible engineers ready to make a difference in the world. |

### Growth and Career opportunities in Mechanical and Mechatronics Engineering

 Thakur College of Engineering and Technology(TCET)’s Mechanical and mechatronics engineering (Additive Manufacturing) degree program was designed around standards set by All India Counsel for Technical Education (AICTE). It is the first and only bachelor’s degree program in Mumbai of Higher Education — your most affordable choice for higher education for growing future.

 TCET’s BE in Mechanical and Mechatronics Engineering (Additive Manufacturing) is established with the view point of emerging technologies and hence its combination well set department of Electronics Engineering started in 2008 and Mechanical Engineering in the year 2012. Having reach experience of Mechanical, Electronics and computer Engineering, Department of Mechanical and Mechatronics started in 2021. Mechatronics engineering technologists use a combination of mechanical, electrical, computer, and software skills to work with smart technologies, such as robots, automated guided systems, and computer-integrated manufacturing.
