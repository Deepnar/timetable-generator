### Activity Based Learning

 ABL is a course for second Year students where students actively participate in the learning experience rather than sit as passive listeners.

 The Objective of ABL course is to prepare students for Leadership and Excellence in their life roles by continually engaging students in both individual and team activities that explore important issues or phenomena, use multiple media and technologies, create products that embody the results of the students explorations, and call the students to explain their work and products to adult and student audience. This activity is conducted in first week of academic start and is evaluated by mentors. This course is theme based and all activities are aligned to the central theme.

 In this course activities such as personality development (TAT WAT &SRT Test), Case Study or Story writing, Lecturette (Extempore speech), Group Discussion, Survey Designing, Blog Writing with a connect to Society, Drama/ skit. etc are conducted by faculty members. All activities are evaluated on individual and team basis by faculty members.

 This course will help them to think and develop presence of mind, handle situations and resolve the problems arising due to given situations also give them wings of imagination with self-expression in the topic. Students also get an opportunity to explore real time challenges and display their talent.
