###  Outcome Based Education
[IMG: obe-etrx]

Outcome-based education (OBE) is an educational approach that focuses on defining specific learning outcomes that students are expected to achieve by the end of a course or program. OBE focuses shifts from merely delivering content to ensuring that students achieve the desired learning outcomes. The teaching and learning process is designed in such a way to support students in achieving the desired outcomes. Assessments are aligned with set outcomes to measure student performance accurately. Rubrics, checklists, and other assessment tools are often used to evaluate students' progress and determine their level of attainment. OBE is closely associated with competencies like knowledge, skills to enhance students' employability. Through ongoing assessment and feedback, professors can identify areas for improvement in curriculum design, instructional strategies, and learning resources. Thus, we can align the engineering education more closely with the need of stakeholders. Thus, in TCET by focusing on specific outcomes, we can produce graduates who are well prepared for the demands of industry and, society.

### Program Educational Objectives

Students of BE Programme in Electronics and Computer Science will be able to:

| PEO 1: | To develop core competencies in the field of Electronics and Computer Science and design, and Professional Skills and Professional Growth. |
| PEO 2: | Solve problems in diverse fields using knowledge of Electronics and Computer Science and Pursue higher education or research, demonstrate entrepreneur quality. |
| PEO 3: | Exhibit adaptability, teamwork, leadership and communication skill required for successful career. |

### Programme Outcomes

| PO 1 | ENGINEERING KNOWLEDGE: Apply knowledge of mathematics, natural science, computing, engineering fundamentals and an engineering specialization as specified respectively to the solution of complex engineering problems. |
| PO 2 | PROBLEM ANALYSIS: Identify, formulate and review research literature to analyze complex engineering problems, reaching substantiated conclusions with consideration for sustainable development. |
| PO 3 | DESIGN / DEVELOPMENT OF SOLUTIONS: Design creative solutions for complex engineering problems and design / develop systems / components / processes to meet identified needs, considering public health and safety, life-cycle cost, net-zero carbon, culture, society and the environment. |
| PO 4 | CONDUCT INVESTIGATIONS OF COMPLEX PROBLEMS: Conduct investigations using research-based knowledge, including design of experiments, modelling and data interpretation, to provide valid conclusions for complex engineering problems. |
| PO 5 | ENGINEERING TOOL USAGE: Create, select and apply appropriate techniques, resources and modern IT tools—including prediction and modelling—while recognizing their limitations, to solve complex engineering problems. |
| PO 6 | THE ENGINEER AND THE WORLD: Analyze and evaluate societal and environmental aspects when solving complex engineering problems, assessing impacts on sustainability in terms of economy, health, safety, legal frameworks, culture and environment. |
| PO 7 | ETHICS: Apply ethical principles and commit to professional ethics, human values, diversity and inclusion, adhering to relevant national and international laws. |
| PO 8 | INDIVIDUAL & COLLABORATIVE TEAM WORK: Function effectively as an individual and as a member or leader in diverse, multidisciplinary teams. |
| PO 9 | COMMUNICATION: Communicate effectively and inclusively within the engineering community and with society at large—comprehending and writing clear reports, designing documentation and making impactful presentations—while respecting cultural, language and learning differences. |
| PO 10 | PROJECT MANAGEMENT & FINANCE: Apply engineering-management principles and sound economic decision-making to one’s own work, to lead teams, manage projects and perform in multidisciplinary environments. |
| PO 11 | LIFE-LONG LEARNING: Recognize the need for, and develop the ability for, independent, life-long learning, adaptability to emerging technologies and critical thinking amid technological change. |

### Program Specific Outcomes

| PSO 1 | Students will be able to understand, analyze, design and implement functional blocks of hardware, software or hardware-software co-design for Electronics and Computer Science applications |
| PSO 2 | Students will be able to design product, modules, interfaces and databases for a system by providing suitable architecture and integrate hardware, software and modern tools technology for hybrid engineering application. |
| PSO 3 | Students will able to communicate with range of audience and to recognize professional and ethical obligations in engineering contexts and to reach well-informed decisions that take into account the effects of engineering solutions in societal, economic, environmental, and global contexts. |
