In brief: All industries today — from healthcare to finance, entertainment to agriculture — are being reimagined by data and AI.

If you're interested in helping create the next generation of intelligent systems, now is your time.

### Demand Is Booming (And Supply Is Low)

 Industry accounts have it that [AI and data science jobs](https://www.tcetmumbai.in) are among the highest-paying and fastest-growing occupations in the world. Job descriptions such as Machine Learning Engineer, Data Scientist, AI Researcher, and Business Intelligence Analyst are no longer unheard of — they're in high demand.

By the Numbers:

- More than 150 million new technology jobs will be created worldwide by 2025.

- The pay for AI professionals is higher than many legacy engineering jobs.

- But there is a huge skill shortage — businesses are desperate to find qualified professionals.

 Translation? You're not merely selecting a career — you're placing yourself as a scarce, high-skilled expert.

In brief: AI does not destroy jobs. It redefines them — and you'll want to be in front of that curve.
