### Local Industrial Visit to Vervali Systems Pvt. Ltd.

 The purpose of this industrial visit was to provide Second-Year (SE) students with hands-on exposure to software development processes, quality assurance methodologies, and the real-world application of industry practices at Vervali Systems Pvt. Ltd., a leading IT solutions provider. This visit aimed to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry requirements.

# Industry Visit Objectives

⦿ To understand the software development life cycle (SDLC) and agile methodologies followed in the IT industry.

⦿ To gain insights into quality assurance and testing processes in software engineering.

⦿ To interact with industry professionals and understand the skill sets required for career opportunities in software development and testing.

⦿ To explore various tools and technologies used for automation and manual testing.

⦿ To comprehend the workflow of real-time IT projects and client handling procedures.

# Outcomes Achieved

⦿ Students acquired practical knowledge of software development, testing, and quality assurance techniques.

⦿ Enhanced understanding of agile practices, DevOps, and industry-standard testing tools.

⦿ Improved awareness of career prospects in software development, testing, and IT consulting.

⦿ Exposure to live projects and real-world problem-solving approaches.

⦿ Strengthened interaction with professionals, fostering industry-academia collaboration.
