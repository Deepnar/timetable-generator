#### About Symposiums

 The New National Education Policy (NEP-2020) announced by the Government of India on 29th July 2020 is the first such policy in last 34 years which is going to transform the future of education in India. The NEP is ambitious and futuristic but most of its success depends on how it is executed. Therefore, discussions and deliberations are required on this to ensure the fruits of this policy are reached to all stakeholders envisioning India as Knowledge Superpower by 2040. TCET’s Internal Quality Assurance Cell  is making an attempt to bring all the stakeholders on a platform to discuss on the implementation of NEP 2020 to ensure the enhanced quality offerings reach to the primary stakeholders swiftly. We're looking forward for a healthy participation during the symposium.

#### Objectives:

 The symposium objectives are :

- To get deeper insights into New Education policy and make roadmap for its swift implementation.

- To discuss & understand the quality practices adopted by the Institutions & Faculty members for benefit of primary stakeholders.

#### Expected Outcomes:

- At the end of the symposium, the participants shall be able to apply the learning to improve the quality of offerings at their respective institutions in the larger interest of stakeholders.
