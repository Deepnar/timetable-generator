 The department of Computer Engineering, started its journey in the Academic Year 2001-02 and is committed to deliver the program with rigor with active industry participation. Department has 180 seats intake at first year and 20 seats as lateral entry at 2nd year for directly admitted diploma students.The department has Post graduate programme with 18 intake from the Academic Year 2011-12 onwards. The department believes in student centric approach. Its dedicated team of faculty members inculcate relevant knowledge, skills and attitude in students to become successful professionals. The U.G. programme is accredited by National Board of Accreditation (NBA), New Delhi for three years w.e.f. 16.09.2011. UG Programme has been re-accredited for 3 years by NBA w.e.f 1st July 2016. Also the programme is permanently affiliated with UOM since AY 2015-16.

# Major Subjects

Basic Engineering: Discrete Structures and Graph Theory, Computer Organizational & Architecture, Analysis and Design of Algorithm etc.

Core Engineering: Data Structures, Digital logic Design Applications, Data Base Management Systems, Operating Systems, Microprocessor, Computer Network, Digital Signal Processing,Image Processing, Theoretical Computer Science, Data warehousing and Mining, Object Oriented Programming Methodology etc.

Advanced Engineering: Advanced Database Management System, Advanced Microprocessor, Advanced Computer Network, Software Engineering, Intelligent Systems, Cryptography and System Security,Parallel & Distributed Computing Advance Algorithms,Computer Simulation and Modeling,Software Architecture, Machine Learning,Embedded Systems,Adhoc wireless networks etc.

Application based : Web Engineering, Computer Graphics, Computer Vision, Aritificial Intelligence, Human Computer Interaction, Mobile Computing, E-Commerce, Distributed Database,ERP and Supply Chain Management,Digital Forensic ,Big data Analytics etc.
