## Institutional Internship

Objective :
 To give students an exposure to the new skills and develop competency, ability to simplify work and people skills. To develop a platform for them to learn these skills from mentors in the college and industry experts, by working in laboratory facility of the institute and the sister concerns. While working for any of the following internships 4H model can be used.
Expected Outcome:
 1. Students will work on institute level projects.
 2. Students will learn industry inspired workflow developed by the TCET Open Source organization and learn to work in collaboration in large teams.
 3. Students will develop skills with respect to software engineering best practices, communication, design & development skills.
