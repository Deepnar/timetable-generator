### TCET / E & TC - IEEE
[IMG: TCET]

 The IEEE-TCET chapter, established in 2003-04, is a dynamic platform for technocrats that’s part of the world’s largest technical professional organization. With over 423,000 members across more than 160 countries, IEEE supports professionals in electrical, electronic, computing, and related fields. Led by the Department of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering, our chapter provides a global network of collaborations through societies like GRSS and MTT-S.

 We organize various professional and educational activities, including seminars, workshops, and events, to enhance students’ technical skills and expand their knowledge. By joining us, members gain access to IEEE’s extensive resources, such as its website and monthly technical magazine, keeping them informed about the latest industry trends. Come and join us to foster innovation and technological excellence for humanity!
