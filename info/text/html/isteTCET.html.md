### TCET / ES&H - ISTE
[IMG: TCET]

The Indian Society for Technical Education (ISTE) is a national, professional, and non- profit society registered under the Societies Registration Act of 1860. As the only national organization of educators in Engineering and Technology, ISTE collaborates with MHRD, AICTE, MIT, and international projects, contributing to government missions and presenting over 70 awards annually to institutions, educators, and students for innovation and excellence.

The ISTE-TCET Students’ Chapter was established in A.Y. 2013-14 with the goal of enhancing co-curricular, extracurricular, and professional development activities for students. Managed by the ES&H Department, the chapter promotes STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics) and aims to bridge the gap between academic curriculum and industry needs.

It provides a platform for students to engage in activities that foster a competitive spirit and keep them updated on the latest technological advancements. The chapter also publishes THE BYTE, a bi-annual e-magazine that gives first-year students exposure to research writing and presentation of both technical and non-technical skills. Currently, over 2040 students are registered under the chapter.

# Future plans

- Organizing Technical Seminars and Workshops: The chapter plans to conduct a series of technical seminars and workshops aimed at helping students explore emerging fields that lie at the intersection of multidisciplinary domains such as culture, education, and technology. These sessions will enhance students’ understanding of current trends and innovations.

- Enhancing Cognitive and Digital Skills through STEM:  Emphasis will be placed on fostering cognitive development, adaptive thinking, and digital literacy among students through engaging STEM learning experiences. These initiatives aim to prepare students effectively for future academic pursuits, professional careers, and responsible participation in community life.

- Industrial Visits, Competitions, and Recreational Events: Future activities will include industrial visits, technical and non-technical competitions, and recreational events. These will provide students with practical exposure to real-world applications of technical concepts and help cultivate entrepreneurial thinking, leadership skills, and team collaboration by bridging theoretical learning with hands-on experience.
