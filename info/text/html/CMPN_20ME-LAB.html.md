### Laboratories

The Department of Computer Engineering (Post graduate) has developed laboratories having all modern configurations. As courses change over time to meet Industry requirements, these laboratories are upgraded to match these requirements. These labs are equipped with the latest technologies to provide an environment of modern teaching –learning to students as well as to faculties. In these labs, students can carry out practical learning session and can do research and development activities also.
 The P.G Lab is equipped with dedicated 20 LAN connected computers, various open source software and high speed Internet connectivity.
 For implementing research based and Industry relevant projects, the Institute has

- Remote centre of IIT Bombay for online research

- Android mobile application development Lab

- e-yantra laboratory

Under Industry-Institute-Interaction the institute has

- Thakur-Accenture Innovation Centre for advance computing and communication
