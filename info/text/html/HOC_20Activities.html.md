## In-Campus Training programs

 For guiding and motivating students to take up various Competitive Examinations like GATE, GRE, IELTS, CAT & TOEFL, HOC cell conducts in campus training program for GATE and also has collaboration with IMS Learning Recourse PVT. Ltd for training of CAT, GRE, TOFEL etc.

| Training Program | Trainers | Purpose |
| GATE | Internal faculty members | M.E./M.Tech /PSU’s |
| GRE, TOEFL | IMS Learning Recourse PVT. Ltd | M.S. |
| CAT,GMAT,CET | IMS Learning Recourse PVT. Ltd | MBA |
