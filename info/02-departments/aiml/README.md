## Vision

Vision |
| To become a department of international relevance in the field of Artificial Intelligence and Machine learning. |

| Mission |
| To nurture students with sound engineering knowledge through the effective use of modern tools with a focus on local and global employability as an AI&ML professional by imbibing leadership qualities, ethical attitude, lifelong learning and social sensitivity. |

| Program Specific Outcomes |

| Life @ AI&ML |

| AI&ML is a fascinating and rapidly evolving field, and with the constant guidance and support of TCET’s dedicated faculty, the learning journey becomes even more engaging and enriching. From an academic perspective, all subjects are taught in a clear and comprehensive manner, integrated with practical applications of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. Even subjects like Environmental Science are explored through real-world case studies, demonstrating how AI&ML algorithms contribute to climate preservation and pollution reduction. The ACM professional body at TCET plays a vital role in enhancing student's exposure by organizing various academic and co-curricular activities from webinars on higher education to major events during Zephyr, the institute’s annual technical festival. Students are also encouraged to showcase and refine their talents through participation in college committees and magazines, whether as organizers or contributors. Overall TCET provides a nurturing environment and ample opportunities for students to develop into skilled and successful Machine Learning professionals. Student are nurtured and given all possible platforms where they can mold themselves as successful Machine Learners. |
