## Vision

Vision

“The department of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering envisions developing internationally competent professionals with a sense of responsibility and social sensitivity.”

## Mission

Mission

1. : To provide a conducive learning environment and resources for fostering the academic knowledge and skills of students so that they become eminent technocrats.

2. : To transform the students into competent professionals capable of providing engineering solutions to societal problems.

3. : To endow students with ethics and human values so that they become responsible citizens with social sensitivity
