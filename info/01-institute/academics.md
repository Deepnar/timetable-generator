## Academic model

- **Choice-Based Credit Grading System (CBCGS)** — current HME (Holistic and
  Multidisciplinary Education) scheme per NEP, e.g. "HME 2023" scheme visible in
  result registers; earlier "HME 2020" scheme.
- **Semester pattern:** odd/even semesters; FE (First Year) common; SE/TE/BE for
  degree years; ME/MTech PG semesters.
- **Assessment:** In-semester examinations (2 per sem), mid-term, end-semester
  (supplementary in July), plus term-work/oral/practical components. Grades A–F
  with GP; GPA/SGPA/CGPI computed (see result registers).
- **OBE** (Outcome Based Education) with PSOs/POs per program; **skill-based** and
  **research-based** education tracks; value-added courses; 4T's integration.
- **Credits:** courses carry credits (e.g. 2–4 per course); holistic courses
  include Industry Certification, Project, Internship, Research-Based Learning.
