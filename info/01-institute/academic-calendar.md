## REVISED ACADEMIC CALENDAR (Tentative) — AY 2026-27, SEM-Odd (June → September 2026)

**Audience:** B.E./B.Tech students, B.Voc (Third Year)
**Commencement of semester:** 8th June 2026

| Weeks | Dates | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 1st–2nd week | 8 June – 20 June 2026 | Induction/Orientation; conduct of regular timetable along with industry readiness training |
| 3rd–5th week | 22 June – 11 July 2026 | Conduct of regular timetable |
| (exam) | 13–15 July 2026 | In-Semester Examination-I / conduct of classes |
| 6th–11th week | 16 July – 21 August 2026 | Conduct of regular timetable |
| 11th–12th week | 22 Aug – 25 Aug 2026 | In-Semester Examination-II / conduct of classes |
| 12th–14th week | 27 Aug – 12 Sep 2026 | Regular timetable in Collaborative learning mode (Open Electives / Course Assignments / Project work / Industry Training / Preparation for Competitive Exams & Higher Studies / Placement Activities / Continuous Assessment) |
| (working day) | 12 September 2026 | Working for all faculty & academic staff |
| 15th week | 15 Sep – 19 Sep 2026 | Ganapati festival (05 days) |
| 16th week | — | — |
| 17th–18th week | — | Training/Placement process |

**Total instructional days: 90 days**

### Rules & notes (from the document)

1. Each faculty member must conduct **minimum 42 lectures and 10 practical/tutorial
   sessions** per semester; HoDs notify and monitor this.
2. On holidays, faculty must hold extra lectures to compensate academic loss.
3. ISE-3 and Innovative Exam conducted in batches throughout the semester.
4. Result declaration: **third week of November 2026**.
   ATKT Exam (all semesters, odd + even): **3rd – 14th August 2026**.
5. Technical festival (**Zephyr**): **29 Sep – 1 Oct 2026**.
6. BE/BT classes in blended learning mode as required.
7. **Commencement of Even Semester AY 2026-27: 2nd January 2027**.

Signed: **(Dr. B.K. Mishra), Principal** — dated 4th June 2026.

> **Project note:** this is the master academic calendar the scheduler must respect:
> exam weeks (13–15 Jul, 22–25 Aug), festival closure (15–19 Sep), the 90-day
> instructional count, and the 42-lecture/10-practical minimum per faculty per
> semester are all hard constraints for timetable generation.
