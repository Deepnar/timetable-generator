# TCET Institute — About, Accreditation, Facts

Source: `about-us.html`, `institutional-growth.html`, `recognition-&-awards.html`, `aboutacademics.html`

## Identity

- **Full name:** Thakur College of Engineering & Technology (TCET), Mumbai
- **Status:** Autonomous institute, linguistic minority institute
- **Established:** AY 2001-02
- **Affiliation:** Permanently affiliated to the University of Mumbai (all programs)
- **Courses offered:** 21 U.G., 11 P.G., and 5 Ph.D. (Tech.) courses
- **Governing parent body:** ZCT — Zakeria (Thakur) charitable trust; Shri. V.K. Singh is Chairman of ZCT

## Educational model

- Outcome-Based Education (OBE) since AY 2008-09 (NBA mandate); NAAC alignment since AY 2017-18
- ISO 9001:2015 QMS since AY 2005-06; Malcolm Baldrige-based performance excellence model since AY 2015-16
- 6S quality model since AY 2017-18
- Bloom's learning model (AY 2012-13); 4H life-skill model (head, heart, hand, health) (AY 2015-16)
- Education delivered across three domains: **Academic, Training, Research**, with curricular + co-curricular + extracurricular + professional activities
- Learning integrated with the "4 T's": Tools, techniques, technology, technology platforms
